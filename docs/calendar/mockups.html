What can appear on a calendar

Event with a time (blue) Another calendar's colour (green) Multi-day / all-day (amber) Task with a due date (dashed, from Tasks)
  • Events: title, date, optional time range, all-day, repeat, reminder.
  • Several calendars (Personal, Work…) like task lists, each with a colour, shown or hidden with chips.
  • Tasks with a due date shown read-only on their day (optional switch), tap opens the task.
  • Plan activities could be shown as multi-day bars later.
  • Synced and offline-first like every other entity; reminders reuse the existing push/ntfy sweeper.
